# National claim outline: source and distribution status

The default Census-2011 state/district geometry is statistical and historical. The optional national reference outline is therefore a **separate current DataMeet-derived overlay**: `generated/datameet-current-claim-outline/outline.topo.json`, object `outline`, one non-interactive `in-datameet-current-claim-outline` feature. It is an openly licensed CC BY 4.0 derivative of DataMeet's `States/Admin2.shp`, produced by dissolving current state/UT outlines and removing their internal arcs.

The overlay is checked against the extent of Survey of India's current political-outline depiction—the relevant standard under the [Government of India geospatial guidelines](https://onlinemaps.surveyofindia.gov.in/GeospatialGuidelines.aspx)—but is **not** Survey of India geometry, a certification, or an administrative/statistical layer. Render it as “reference outline; data unavailable”, non-interactively, and never use it for values or joins.

Survey of India supplies an official international-boundary vector through its [Outline Maps page](https://www.surveyofindia.gov.in/pages/outline-maps-of-india). We do not distribute, transform, or use it in the product because its published [usage notice](https://surveyofindia.gov.in/documents/ibd-vectorformat.pdf) identifies it as an SoI copyright/IPR product and prohibits commercial use. That restriction is incompatible with an MIT/open-source package that permits commercial redistribution.

The reproducible DataMeet transformation and its manifest record the upstream commit, input checksum, CC BY 4.0 attribution, processing method, output checksum, and extent. It validates that the overlay reaches the expected western, northern, eastern and southern political-outline envelope. This validates geographic coverage—not official status—and keeps the statistical Census layer untouched.

For the historical Census-2011 `in-hs-01-jammu-and-kashmir` drill-down, `historical-parent-overlays/in-hs-01-jammu-and-kashmir.topo.json` is a distinct merged reference outline of DataMeet's current **Jammu & Kashmir + Ladakh** source features. It covers the full current claimed-territory reference context while the 22 historical Census districts remain the only value-bearing geometry. Render the added extent hatched and data-unavailable; never infer, impute, or aggregate a district value for it.
