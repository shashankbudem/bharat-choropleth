What the numbers are

  • Female literacy — Census of India 2011, Primary Census Abstract. State and district, on the 2011 boundaries.
  • Sanitation, vaccination, stunting, anaemia — NFHS-5 (2019–21) state factsheets, on current boundaries.
  • Groundwater extraction — CGWB Dynamic Ground Water Resources of India, 2023, on current boundaries.

Every figure is published official data joined to boundary bundles in this repository, with the producer, download URL, source SHA-256 and join rule shown in each page. Nothing is estimated: where a source does not cover a region — or cannot be honestly joined to it — the region reads No data.
