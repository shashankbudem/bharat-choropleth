BharatChoropleth — one script tag, one object

No stylesheet link, no build step, no await. The script tag injects its own CSS; boundary data is fetched in the background and values set before it lands are applied when it arrives.

Hover or tab through the map. The tooltip shows the value, its share of the total and its rank. Values below are made up, for demonstration only. Two states are left unset to show how “no data” reads.

State/UT and district boundaries derived from datta07/INDIAN-SHAPEFILES (MIT).
